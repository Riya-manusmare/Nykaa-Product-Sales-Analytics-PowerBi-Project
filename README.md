# Nykaa Dataset Analysis & Visualization 📊

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing the **Nykaa e-commerce dataset** to uncover insights related to product performance, brand contribution, pricing strategy, discounts, ratings, and customer reviews.  
The analysis is presented through **interactive Power BI dashboards** for clear and actionable business insights.

---

## 🧾 Dataset Summary
- **Total Products:** 4,856  
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 27K  
- **Total Ratings:** 4M  
- **Total Reviews:** 708K  
- **Total Selling Price:** 5.02M  
- **Total Cost Price:** 3.87M  
- **Margin Amount:** 1.15M  

---

## 🎯 Key Business Questions Answered
- Which **categories and brands** contribute the most to sales?
- How do **ratings and reviews** impact product performance?
- What is the **cost vs selling price** trend across categories?
- Which brands have the **highest margins**?
- How do **discounts** affect pricing and sales?
- What is the **gender-wise product distribution**?

---

## 📊 Dashboards & Visualizations
- 📦 Quantity by Category & Brand  
- ⭐ Top 10 Products by Ratings  
- 🧴 Category-wise Selling Price Distribution  
- 💸 Cost vs Selling Price Analysis  
- 📈 Margin % Across Categories & Brands  
- 🗣️ Ratings vs Reviews Scatter Analysis  
- 👤 Gender Categorization (Men / Women / Unisex)  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
- **Power BI** – Data modeling, DAX measures, interactive dashboards  
- **Excel** – Data cleaning and preprocessing

## 📈 Key Insights
- Skincare and Makeup dominate overall sales and ratings  
- Premium brands show higher margins despite moderate discounts  
- Strong positive correlation between **ratings and reviews**  
- Balanced contribution from Men, Women, and Unisex categories

## 🚀 Conclusion
This project demonstrates the ability to convert raw e-commerce data into **meaningful insights using Power BI**, helping businesses make data-driven decisions related to pricing, inventory, and marketing strategy.
